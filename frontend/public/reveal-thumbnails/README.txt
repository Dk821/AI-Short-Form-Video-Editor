Reveal Animation thumbnails
============================

The "Edit Transition" popup (Reveal Animation picker) shows one image
per animation style as the card's background. Drop your own image in
here to replace a placeholder — keep the exact same filename (must end
in .jpg — convert/export as .jpg if your source is a different format).

Required filenames (one per animation id):

  none.jpg
  slide_down.jpg
  slide_up.jpg
  slide_left.jpg
  slide_right.jpg
  fade_in.jpg
  zoom_in.jpg
  pop.jpg
  wipe_down.jpg
  bounce_in.jpg

Recommended size: a portrait (9:16) frame, e.g. 360x640 or larger —
same aspect ratio as the vertical video preview. Any size works; it's
displayed with object-fit: cover.

The picker plays the actual reveal animation (slide/fade/zoom/wipe/
bounce) on top of whatever image is here, so a real frame from your
footage will show the effect the same way it looks in the real
preview/export — not just a generic label card.
